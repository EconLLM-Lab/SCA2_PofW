\section*{Code, data, and disclosures}
The repository is \url{https://github.com/EconLLM-Lab/SCA2_PofW}. The reported correlations are reprinted from the committed country--item score file in that repository. The zip linked here is an earlier archive of model outputs from 17 September 2026 and is not the source of the reported trust correlation of 0.74. It contains no survey microdata, at \url{https://drive.google.com/uc?export=download&id=1lIAx0ueSpgaZPmAzNbFSD31ddGQH7Nqo}. Adapter weights are released at \url{https://huggingface.co/Bonorinoa/SCA2-phase2-adapters}, commit \texttt{03c43dfd27c9535283feb78ffa727da3bfa1b966}. A Hugging Face account and acceptance of the repository gate are required to download them. Reprinting the reported tables uses the committed score file. Scoring new item text uses the adapters.

GPS and WVS data remain subject to their providers' access and use conditions \cite{falk2018,wvs2022}. The package does not redistribute respondent microdata. The analysis is at the country level and should not be used to infer an individual's preferences or to portray national populations as homogeneous. Independent human work would require its own consent and ethics procedures.

The study was not preregistered. Common-support corrections, the forty-two-country human map, and reported sensitivity checks are retrospective. The authors declare no funding and no competing interests. Language models were used for writing and coding assistance. The authors remain responsible for the claims, analysis, and final manuscript.
