\section*{Code, data, and disclosures}
The project repository is \url{https://github.com/EconLLM-Lab/SCA2_PofW}. Headline tables are regenerated from frozen country--item scores by \texttt{analysis/phase2/22\_paper\_a\_surface.py}. Option-probability files used in scoring are posted as a zip of model outputs only, without survey microdata, at \url{https://drive.google.com/uc?export=download&id=1lIAx0ueSpgaZPmAzNbFSD31ddGQH7Nqo}. An unauthenticated fetch of that zip succeeded at the time of writing. Adapter weights reside in the Hugging Face repository \texttt{Bonorinoa/SCA2-phase2-adapters} and remain private until the authors make that repository public; they are optional for regenerating the locked tables and are required only to score new item text.

GPS and WVS data remain subject to their providers' access and use conditions \cite{falk2018,wvs2022}. This manuscript package does not redistribute respondent microdata. The analysis is at country level and should not be used to infer an individual's preferences or to portray national populations as homogeneous. Independent human work would require its own consent and ethics procedures.

The study was not preregistered. The original draft describes an analysis plan written before outcome inspection; the common-support corrections, the forty-two-country human map, and reported sensitivity checks are retrospective. The supplied draft declares no funding and no competing interests; those declarations require author confirmation before submission. Language models are the scientific object of the study and were also used for writing and coding assistance. The authors remain responsible for the claims, analysis, and final manuscript.
