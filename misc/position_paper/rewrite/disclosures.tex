\section*{Code, data, and disclosures}
The repository is \url{https://github.com/EconLLM-Lab/SCA2_PofW}. Headline tables are regenerated from the country--item score file by \texttt{analysis/phase2/22\_paper\_a\_surface.py}. The posted zip is model outputs only, without survey microdata, at \url{https://drive.google.com/uc?export=download&id=1lIAx0ueSpgaZPmAzNbFSD31ddGQH7Nqo}. Adapter weights are not public; they are optional for regenerating the headline tables and are required only to score new item text.

GPS and WVS data remain subject to their providers' access and use conditions \cite{falk2018,wvs2022}. The package does not redistribute respondent microdata. The analysis is at the country level and should not be used to infer an individual's preferences or to portray national populations as homogeneous. Independent human work would require its own consent and ethics procedures.

The study was not preregistered. Common-support corrections, the forty-two-country human map, and reported sensitivity checks are retrospective. The authors declare no funding and no competing interests. Language models were used for writing and coding assistance. The authors remain responsible for the claims, analysis, and final manuscript.
