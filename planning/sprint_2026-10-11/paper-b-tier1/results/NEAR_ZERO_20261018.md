# Near-zero margin refit — C6 sensitivity (2026-10-18)

Card **T7**. The predeclared near-zero refits (card T5) read against their size-matched controls
(C6_DESIGN.md section 9). Every number below was produced by `paper_b/analyze_near_zero.py` from its
cited outputs; nothing is typed by hand. This is a sensitivity on the sign rule's margin, reported
beside the headline — it is not a new estimand and it is never promoted to a claim.

## 1. What was compared

For each predeclared country, the refit adapter differs from the baseline profile adapter in two
things at once: whole near-zero dimensions leave training, *and* the dataset is smaller. The
size-matched control (same unit, same number of training rows, drawn uniformly at random, every
dimension eligible) holds the data size fixed, so the **retained-dimension** contrast isolates *which*
rows were removed. Following C6_DESIGN.md section 9:

    primary C6 contrast   (refit composite - control composite) / SD_s
    data-size reference   (refit - baseline) / SD_s  and  (control - baseline) / SD_s

with the composite the frozen-recode item mean (`analyze_factorial.unit_composites`) and SD_s the SD
(ddof 1) of the 64 profile composites of the bank-2 strict run (`adapter_strict_v1.csv`).

Blocks (C6_DESIGN.md section 5, fixed before any refit was trained):

- **Block R** — surfaces whose dimension is *still in training* after the removal. The C6 rule is
  applied here, on the imprint surfaces.
- **Block D** — dropped dimensions. A loss-of-signal check: by construction these move toward the
  untrained behaviour, so a move here is *not* evidence for or against the filter.

Imprint surfaces: trust, risktaking, posrecip, altruism. Intended direction of a surface is the unit's
own sign bit (up for 1, down for 0). The noise floor is the replicate's |delta| on the same surface
(C6_DESIGN.md section 9, item 4).

## 4. The reading (C6_DESIGN.md section 9, items 4-5)

Applied to the **block-R imprint surfaces only** (GBR trust, COL posrecip), against the replicate's
noise floor:

| Country | Surface | Intended | (refit-control)/SD | floor | beyond floor | against intended |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| GBR | trust | up | +0.4477 | 0.0249 | yes | **no** |
| COL | posrecip | up | −0.0923 | 0.0114 | yes | **yes** |

**COL posrecip moves against its intended direction beyond the noise floor.** GBR trust moves strongly
in the intended direction. Following the rule as written:

> **The near-zero filter is not promoted.** A retained imprint surface (COL posrecip) moves against its
> intended direction beyond the retraining noise floor, so the C6 margin does not survive as a
> changeless footnote — it is reported as a reversal on that surface.

This is the screen reading, not a verdict on the estimand: no interval is claimed and two refits cannot
resolve the sign of a 0.09-SD move. The whole point of the pre-registered rule is that this reversal is
reported as such and the filter is *not* promoted; the headline (adapter–GPS on the seven surfaces)
stands unchanged, with the near-zero margin noted as failing its own sensitivity screen.

## 5. Block D (loss of signal), not evidence

Block D is reported only to show what removal does, never to promote or reject the filter. Four of the
seven dropped-dimension moves go against the profile's intended direction beyond the floor — GBR
negrecip −0.7514 SD, COL trust −0.4805 SD (the two largest block-D moves), GBR posrecip +0.2192 SD and
COL risktaking +0.0859 SD — which is the loss-of-signal pattern C6_DESIGN.md section 5 predicted for a
dropped imprint dimension. The other three do not (GBR altruism +0.2417, COL altruism +0.0618, GBR
risktaking −0.0178, the last within its floor). None of these enters the decision, because a dimension
with no training rows moves toward the untrained behaviour by construction.

## 7. Not certain

- Two refits are a screen. No interval, no p-value, no across-profile claim: one singleton and one
  multi-member country cannot speak for the 42.
- The noise floor is one unseeded retrain of one unit (`P_1_0_1_1_0_1`), so it is thin and is not the
  across-unit retraining variance.
- `patience:Q43` is at the ceiling with a small SD; its deltas are reported for completeness only.
- Block membership and the intended direction come from the T3 subset declaration and the unit bit
  pattern, not from any score.

## 8. Orchestrator review (2026-10-17): the declared floor is too small for small-data refits

All numbers in sections 3 and 4 were re-derived by the orchestrator from the Hub score files with separate
code (`/Users/bonorinoa/.hermes/profiles/sca/cache/scratch/check_t7.py`): exact match. The rule was applied
as declared, and its consequence stands: **the near-zero filter is not promoted.**

The reading needs one qualification, and it concerns the orchestrator's own design choice (C6_DESIGN.md
section 9, item 4). The noise floor came from one unseeded retrain on the FULL 526 training rows. The
size-matched controls show that training on a random 164- or 260-row subset moves the surfaces far more
than that: (control - baseline)/SD ranges up to 1.22 SD for GBR (patience:Q50) and 1.50 SD for COL
(altruism), against floors of 0.01 to 0.11 SD. Against that subsample variation, the COL posrecip move of
-0.092 SD is not distinguishable from zero. The paper therefore states the result as: under the
predeclared rule the near-zero filter is not promoted; the one retained imprint surface that moved against
its intended direction (COL posrecip, -0.09 SD) moved by much less than the variation between two small-data
trainings, so no reversal is claimed. GBR trust moved +0.45 SD in its intended direction, which is also within
that variation. The margin stays a footnote with both facts.
